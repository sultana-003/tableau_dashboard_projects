# 🚗 Car Sales Analysis Dashboard – Tableau Project

## Business Problems

#### The dealership management wants to answer the following business questions:

- What is the total YTD revenue generated?
- How many cars were sold YTD?
- What is the average selling price YTD?
- Which body style generates the highest revenue?
- Which color is most preferred by customers?
- Which company (brand) is performing best?
- How are weekly sales trending?
- Are there demographic differences in purchase behavior? (Assumption-based)

### Project Introduction

#### This project analyzes car sales data from multiple dealers across different regions. The dataset contains information about:

- Annual Income
- Dealer Name
- Company (Brand)
- Model
- Engine Type
- Transmission
- Color
- Price ($)
- Body Style
- Dealer Region

#### The goal of this dashboard is to track Year-To-Date (YTD) performance, identify top-performing segments, and understand sales distribution across different dimensions like body style, color, and brand.

## KPIs
- YTD Total Sales
- YTD Car Sales (Volume)
- YTD Average Price

## Dashboard Charts

- YTD Weekly Sales Trend (Dual Axis – Line + Area)
- YTD Sales by Body Style ( Pie Chart)
- YTD Sales by Color ( Donut Chart)
- YTD Sales by Dealer region
- Company-wise Sales Trend (Text Chart)

 ## Key Insights 

- SUV and Passenger body styles generate higher revenue.
- Pale White and Black are dominant color choices.
- Brands like Toyota and Cadillac appear multiple times, contributing strongly to total revenue.
- Some high-value models significantly increase total sales.
- Dealers in regions like Austin and Scottsdale show frequent transactions.

## Recommendations
####  1. Focus on High Revenue Body Styles
- SUV and Passenger categories generate strong sales. Increase inventory.

####  2. Stock Popular Colors
- Pale White and Black are highly preferred. Optimize procurement accordingly.

####  3. Promote High-Value Brands
- Brands like Toyota, Cadillac, and Infiniti show strong price positioning.

####  4. Regional Marketing Strategy
- Regions with higher activity (Austin, Scottsdale) should receive targeted marketing.

## Project Summary

#### This Tableau dashboard provides a complete Year-To-Date sales overview for multi-brand car dealerships.
#### It enables:

- Performance tracking
- Product category comparison
- Brand analysis
- Sales trend monitoring
- Strategic decision-making
### The dashboard helps management quickly identify revenue drivers and customer preferences.
